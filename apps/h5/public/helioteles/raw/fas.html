Fundo de Arquitectura Social
Visual Identity

Freelance
2014















Fundo de Arquitectura Social (FAS), is a Lisbon-based collective for social architecture. The grassroots organization brings together community leaders, architects, sociologists and other professionals to develop initiatives to better different neighbourhoods of Lisbon. The challenge was to develop a brand identity that embodies the DIY nature of the organization while engaging community members in the creation of communication materials. A simple and flexible identity was designed, so that the community can create, produce and distribute on brand materials.

Design: Hélio Teles

Hélio Teles (b. 1982, Lisbon), is a Montreal-based multidisciplinary designer bridging strategy and design.
